Who am I?

I am Erick Barron and I have always been passionate about videogames, technology and programming. However, my journey into the world of game development started back in 2023 when, after realizing that my current career path wasn't fulfilling me, I decided to take the leap and pursue my passion for game development. Having only made small websites, this was a big change for me — I was ready to learn new skills and challenge myself.

Now I find myself with a strong understanding of not only game development, but also programming in general. This allowed me to create multiple personal projects and work in the industry. This has been a great experience and I have learned a lot from it. There is still a lot to learn, but I am excited to see where this journey takes me.
